FAQ

Good questions.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Call or text (352) 857-9472 for a free estimate. Texting a photo of the tree is a great way to start.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Florida State Tree & Landscaping LLC is licensed and insured. References are available on request.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Belleview, near Summerfield, and serve Marion County, Sumter County, Lady Lake, The Villages and the surrounding areas.

Do you handle storm damage and emergency tree work?

Yes. Storm cleanup and emergency tree work is one of our core services, including hazardous and fallen trees. Our Google listing shows us as open 24 hours.

Do you clean up after the job?

Yes. Reviewers mention the crew stays to get all the debris up, hauls it away, and even blows debris off roofs and gutters.

Do you work on palm trees?

Yes. Palms come up often in our reviews, from trimming to removal. One homeowner had us remove five palms and three pines over the years.

What services do you offer?

Tree removal, tree trimming and pruning, stump grinding, storm cleanup and emergency tree work, tree assessments, and landscaping, including sod.

What phone number should I use?

Our main line is (352) 857-9472. Our Facebook page also lists (352) 454-9841. You can call or text either one.
